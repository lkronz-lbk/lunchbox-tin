# Filming the app on your own phone

Step-by-step for the four screen recordings and the five real-life shots that
go with them. Captions and timings for each reel are in
[launch-copy.md](launch-copy.md); this is how to get the footage.

Budget about **an hour once**, and you will have enough to cut reels into
November. `node scripts/store-clips.mjs` records the same four walks from the
app automatically, to the same recipe — shoot both and use whichever take is
better.

---

## Before anything: the one thing that ruins a take

On a **first run only**, the app offers to sign you in between the last setup
question and the week appearing. If you film onboarding from cold, that screen
lands exactly where the payoff should be.

So: **never film the reveal from onboarding.** Film it from **Shuffle** on the
week screen, which redraws the whole week with one tap and can be repeated as
many times as you like. It looks the same and it is the same thing happening.

(If you ever do want the three questions on camera, open the app in a *private*
browser tab so it runs first-time, film the questions only, and stop the take
the moment you answer the last one. Splice the week on from a Shuffle take.)

## 1 · Set the phone up (five minutes, once)

- **Add the app to the home screen** and open it from the icon. Opened from the
  icon it runs full-screen with no browser address bar. This is the single
  biggest difference between footage that looks like an app and footage that
  looks like a website.
- **Focus / Do Not Disturb on.** One notification banner ruins a take.
- **Auto-Lock → Never** (Settings → Display & Brightness). Nothing worse than
  the screen dimming mid-scroll.
- **Low Power Mode off** — it dims the display and can drop the frame rate.
- **Brightness up**, and clean the screen.
- **Screen Recording in Control Center**: Settings → Control Center → add
  Screen Recording. Swipe down from the top-right, tap the record button, wait
  for the three-second countdown, then start tapping. Red pill at the top to
  stop.

Portrait, always. Don't rotate.

## 2 · Set the app up (five minutes, once)

Make a demo lunchbox you are happy to show the internet:

- A **nickname or initials**, never your child's full name. "Emma" is what the
  screenshots use.
- Open the **lunchbox settings** (the sliders icon at the top right, beside the
  name chip) and set School rules so they're interesting: **Cold lunches only**
  on, **Nuts** kept out. Leave **No ice pack**, **Short eating time** and
  **Dairy** OFF — you will switch those on *on camera*.
- Turn **"They pick their box each day"** on, in the Emma's say section. That is
  what puts the *Let Emma pick* button on the Pack tab.
- Go to the **WEEK** tab and tap **Shuffle** so a full week exists.
- Make sure the **FOODS** list is healthy — if the week keeps drawing the same
  sandwich, add a few more mains and sides first. A repetitive week is honest
  but it films badly.

## 3 · How to take every shot

Same discipline on all of them:

1. Start recording. **Count two seconds before you touch anything.**
2. Tap **deliberately** and **pause 1–2 seconds** after every tap. It will feel
   painfully slow while you're doing it and look right on playback.
3. Scroll with **one slow flick, then stop**. Never flick-flick-flick.
4. **Hold still for two seconds** at the end before you stop recording.
5. Do **three takes** of each. Keep the one where you didn't hesitate.

Those two-second handles at each end are what let you cut cleanly.

## 4 · The four screen recordings

### Clip A — The week draws itself *(the important one)*

Start on the **WEEK** tab, scrolled to the top, with a week already showing.

| # | Do this | Then |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Start recording, hands off | wait 2s |
| 2 | Tap **Shuffle** (green, top right) | hold 3s while the week redraws |
| 3 | Slow scroll down one screen | pause 2s |
| 4 | Slow scroll down one more | pause 2s |
| 5 | Stop | — |

**About 14 seconds.** This is the clip that does the work: it is the whole
product in one gesture.

### Clip B — School rules *(the differentiator)*

Start on the **WEEK** tab.

| # | Do this | Then |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Start recording, hands off | wait 2s |
| 2 | Tap the **sliders icon** (top right) | pause 2s |
| 3 | Scroll to **School rules** | pause 2s |
| 4 | Tap **Short eating time** | pause 2s |
| 5 | Tap **No ice pack** | pause 2s |
| 6 | Scroll down to **Keep out of Emma's box** | pause 1s |
| 7 | Tap **Dairy** | **hold 3s** — a message appears saying how many compartments broke the rules and that it drew them again |
| 8 | Tap **Done** | pause 2s |
| 9 | Slow scroll through the redrawn week | hold 2s |
| 10 | Stop | — |

**About 20 seconds.** Step 7 is the shot. That message is the product's whole
argument and you cannot script it — let it sit on screen long enough to read.

Afterwards, switch Short eating time, No ice pack and Dairy back off so the
demo box is ready for the next take.

### Clip C — The shopping list

Start on the **WEEK** tab.

| # | Do this | Then |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Start recording, hands off | wait 2s |
| 2 | Tap **SHOP** in the bottom bar | pause 2s |
| 3 | Let the list sit — the aisle headings are the point | 2s |
| 4 | Slow scroll down one screen | pause 2s |
| 5 | Tap the checkbox on three items, one at a time | pause 1s between each |
| 6 | Let the counter at the top settle | hold 3s |
| 7 | Stop | — |

**About 16 seconds.** Watch the counter at the top — "20 to buy · 0 already
home" becoming "17 to buy · 3 already home" is the satisfying bit.

Afterwards, uncheck them for the next take.

### Clip D — Kid's pick

Start on the **PACK** tab.

| # | Do this | Then |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Start recording, hands off | wait 2s |
| 2 | Tap **Let Emma pick** | pause 2s |
| 3 | Tap one of the two big tiles | pause 2s |
| 4 | Repeat for the next three screens | pause 2s each |
| 5 | Let the final screen sit | hold 3s |
| 6 | Stop | — |

**About 20 seconds.** If you can film this one over a child's shoulder with
their hand doing the tapping, do — **hands only, no face, no real name.**

## 5 · The five real-life shots

These are what make it a reel instead of a demo. Phone camera, portrait, shot
on something other than the phone you're recording on, or shot at a different
time. Two to four seconds each.

1. **Empty lunchboxes on the counter**, lids off, morning light. This is the
   opening frame of the "week three" reel.
2. **A hand holding the phone at the kitchen counter**, app open, boxes in the
   background and out of focus.
3. **The phone going across the table** — your hand to a smaller hand. Hands
   only.
4. **Packing a box**, close, one item going in at a time.
5. **A packed box closing**, or going into a bag by the door.

And the one that is not a shot list item, because it only needs you and a
kitchen: **thirty seconds to camera about why you built it.** One take, no
script, the 7am scramble and the same four sandwiches. That clip will
out-perform everything else here and nobody else can make it.

---

## 6 · Before you put the phone down

Check each take for:

- A **notification banner** across the top
- Your own **email address** or a real child's full name anywhere on screen
- The **Account** tab opening by accident
- A **hesitation** — a tap that missed, a pause in the wrong place
- The screen **dimming** part way through

If you got all four screen clips plus the five real-life shots, stop. That is
enough footage for six reels, and the next thing worth your time is the
testimonials.
