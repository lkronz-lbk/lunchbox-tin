# Lunch Sorted — Pinterest keyword clusters

**Source:** the content pipeline app, topic 17 (school lunchbox prep), research run
13 September 2026.
**How to use:** before finalising any pin title, confirm the lead term appears
below. Same rule as the Lila Bloom cluster file — this is the Lunch Sorted one,
kept separate because it is a different brand, a different account and different
boards.

**What the tags mean.** ↑ rising · seasonal · steady. There are **no search-volume
numbers here on purpose**: the Pinterest public API exposes none, and a number
read off a trends page would be unverifiable. Tier position and momentum are the
ranking. (That reasoning is the pipeline's own, from migration `0048_seo_research_tiers`.)

## What we can't honestly claim

Three rising terms describe things the app does not do. **Do not chase them with
copy the product can't back**; a pin that wins a click and then disappoints is
worse than no pin.

- `freezer lunchbox prep kids` and `batch lunchbox prep kids` — there is no batch
  or freezer feature. Ignore unless one is ever built.
- `budget lunchbox ideas` — the research ties this to rising cafeteria costs. The
  planner doesn't price anything or optimise for cost. The *honest* adjacent
  claim is that packing is cheaper than buying, which is true but isn't ours to
  take credit for. Leave it unless the shopping list ever carries prices.

The two lanes the research calls wide open that we *can* own outright:
**nut-free / allergen-aware** (rules are a real feature) and **kid-led**
(the kid's-pick screen is genuinely unusual). Both are now pins.

The anti-perfectionism line — *"simple and repeated is fine"* — is worth taking
seriously as positioning, not just a keyword. It is the opposite of the
aspirational bento roundups we are competing against, and it is what the product
actually is.

## Instagram hashtags

From the same run. **Five maximum per post**, the pipeline's own rule.

| Tag | Tier |
|---|---|
| `#lunchbox` `#lunchboxideas` `#schoollunch` `#backtoschool` `#kidslunchbox` | broad |
| `#schoollunchideas` `#lunchboxmom` `#bentolunch` `#lunchboxlove` `#packedlunch` `#healthylunchbox` | mid |
| `#lunchboxinspo` `#funlunch` `#yumbox` | niche |

Two gaps in that set, both worth filling by hand:

- **No allergy tags.** `#nutfreelunch`, `#nutfreeschool`, `#foodallergymom` are
  unvalidated by the pipeline but obviously relevant to our best angle. Treat as
  a guess until a run covers them.
- **No local tags.** Always spend one of the five on the town, school or district
  tag. For a launch that came out of a local group, that one tag is worth more
  than the other four.
