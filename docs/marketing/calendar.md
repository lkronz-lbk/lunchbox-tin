# The calendar — September 2026 to August 2027

Sized for six to eight hours a week, most of it in one block on a Sunday. The
rule that makes the whole thing work: **a channel that gets a gap loses more
than it gained.** Five pins a week for fifty weeks beats twenty pins a week
until November.

The three dates everything hangs on:

| When | What | Why then |
|---|---|---|
| **Mid-January 2027** | The semester push — the rehearsal launch | School restarts, lunchboxes restart, the year's resolution is still alive |
| **March–April 2027** | The iPhone app on the App Store, Product Hunt | Well clear of the peak, so a rejection or a bad build costs nothing |
| **Mid-July to early September 2027** | The launch that matters | Search interest for lunchboxes and back-to-school peaks around the start of August (see README, source 1) |

---

## Phase 1 — Build the engine · September to December 2026

Nobody is watching. That is the point: everything made now is indexed, pinned
and dated by the time anyone is.

**September**
- Turn on Netlify Analytics; add the `/pin`, `/pta`, `/ph` landing redirects.
- Set up the Lunch Sorted Pinterest account: five boards, keyword-led
  descriptions, using the existing keyword-cluster method.
- Build six Canva pin templates in the four compartment colours.
- Write and publish the **first three guides**.
- One screen-recording afternoon → twelve clips banked. Do this once; it
  supplies the whole year.
- Ask the first ten households for a sentence.
- *Cadence from here: 5–10 fresh pins a week, one short video a week, one
  guide a fortnight.*

**October**
- Guides four to seven. Cross-post two to Medium.
- First Facebook-group and Reddit presence: **answer questions only**, no
  links, for the whole month. Introduce yourself to two group moderators and
  ask what is allowed.
- Draft the school/PTA one-pager; send it to one school — Liz's own.
- Write the App Store name, subtitle and 100-character keyword field; check the
  limits in App Store Connect rather than in a blog post.

**November**
- **Pin the January content now** — 45–90 days before the semester peak.
  "Back to school after the break", "lunch rut", "start the term sorted".
- Guides eight to ten.
- TestFlight with ten real parents, most of them from the trial list.
- Waitlist email #1: what is coming, and one honest ask for a reply.

**December**
- The quiet month. Keep pinning; write nothing new.
- Prepare the January push in full: pins scheduled, two videos cut, the
  waitlist email written, the guide updated.
- Review the year's numbers on `/admin`: households, trials, paid, lapsed.

## Phase 2 — The rehearsal · January to February 2027

Treat this as a real launch, at a size where mistakes are cheap.

**Week 1 of January**
- Waitlist email #2: "the planner is free, the new term starts Monday".
- Daily pins for two weeks (the November batch, released now).
- Two videos in the first week: "the first week back" and "nut-free, no
  microwave".
- One post in each parenting community where a moderator has said yes.
- The PTA one-pager to five schools.

**Rest of January and February**
- Watch the trial cohort all the way to day 21. **The number that matters is
  trial-to-paid, and roughly 9% is the honest benchmark for a trial that takes
  no card** (README, source 10).
- Ask every household that reaches week three for a testimonial. Target: twenty.
- Fix whatever the cohort shows. If they never drew a second week, that is a
  product problem and it outranks every marketing task on this page.

---

## Phase 3 — The App Store · March to May 2027

- **March:** submit. Expect one rejection; leave time for it. First three
  screenshots carry the pitch; the 15–30 second preview is cut from the
  September clips. Remember the product rule: the listing sells a free
  planner, and the plan is only ever bought on the web.
- **April:** Product Hunt, on a Tuesday or Wednesday, live at 12:01am Pacific,
  with four weeks of preparation and no upvote-asking. Same week: the press
  one-pager to local parenting newsletters and two or three parenting
  podcasts.
- **May:** two custom product pages (one for Pinterest traffic, one for
  schools). Ask in-app for reviews — only after a second packed week.
- **Late May:** start pinning back-to-school content. This is 45–90 days ahead
  of August, and it is the single most important date in this document.

---

## Phase 4 — The launch that matters · June to September 2027

**June**
- Refresh every guide with a 2027 date and anything learned.
- Write the three back-to-school guides that will carry the season.
- Two videos a week from here.

**July**
- Daily pins. This is the month Pinterest is planning August.
- Waitlist email #3: the app is out, the new year is coming.
- School outreach round two — to district PTA lists, in July, while people are
  planning and not yet drowning.

**August — the peak**
- Everything at once: daily pins, three videos a week, the communities where a
  presence has been earned since October, the schools, the newsletters.
- One "the night before the first day" push in the last week of the month.

**September 2027**
- Stop. Count. The cohort that arrived in August is the year's answer: how many
  households, how many drew a second week, how many paid.
- Then write the next twelve months from what actually happened, not from this.

## The weekly block

Two hours on a Sunday, and it does not move:

1. Make the week's pins (45 min) — templates, keyword-led titles, real links.
2. Cut one video from the banked clips (30 min).
3. Answer three questions in a parent community, link nothing (20 min).
4. Read the `/admin` numbers; write one line in a running log (10 min).
5. Ask one household for a sentence (5 min).

A fortnightly extra two hours writes the next guide. That is the entire plan.
