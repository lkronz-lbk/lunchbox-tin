# App Store keyword check, 2026-10-23

Two days after launch (live 2026-10-21, id 6810858256, 0 ratings). The same analysis the ASO
Scout teaser ran, done from Apple's public search API and the competitors' live pages. Ranks are
the position in Apple's search API for the US storefront, top 200 only; the App Store app orders
slightly differently, and a two-day-old app with no ratings ranks low on every term regardless.
Apple's own statement of what counts: text relevance (title, subtitle, keywords, primary
category) and user behavior (downloads, ratings, reviews). https://developer.apple.com/app-store/search/

Reading it: the app ranks only where a word from the title or subtitle meets a word from the
keyword field ("school lunch" + "planner", "packed" + "lunch"). It is invisible on every term
where the keyword field has to carry the match alone, and on every one-word generic. Six of the
fourteen keywords (snack, grocery, shopping, list, allergy, recipe) describe features no parent
searches an app store for and sit under apps with tens of thousands of ratings.

## Recommendation

Title, subtitle and keywords change only with a new version, so this rides the next build.

| Field | Proposed | Used |
|---|---|---|
| Title | Lunch Sorted: Lunchbox Planner | 30 / 30 |
| Subtitle | Kids' school lunch ideas | 24 / 30 |
| Keywords | box,bento,packed,meal,week,menu,picky,eater,snack,grocery,list,allergy,nut,free,recipe,kindergarten | 99 / 100 |

Why: the title is the heaviest field and had 18 characters empty; "lunchbox" and "planner" move
into it from the keyword field. The subtitle gains "ideas" and "kids" ("Kids' school lunch ideas", Liz's pick on 2026-10-28, since "for kids" reads as who the app is for) and keeps "school" and
"lunch"; it drops "planned", which duplicates "planner". Apple says not to repeat a word across
the three fields and handles plurals itself, so the keyword field loses lunchbox, planner, kids
and shopping (shopping list is a category we will never rank in; grocery + list stays for
"grocery list" in combination with lunch). It gains box (for "lunch box" two words), week and
menu ("weekly lunch menu"), nut + free, and kindergarten, the age the description opens on.
"healthy" is left out on purpose: the handbook says the app never claims it.

Phrases this set can match: lunchbox planner, lunch box planner, school lunch planner, school
lunch ideas, kids lunch ideas, lunchbox ideas, packed lunch ideas, picky eater lunch, kids
lunch menu, weekly lunch menu, nut free lunch, kindergarten lunch, bento box lunch, lunch grocery list.


## What text cannot fix

Ratings. Apple weighs downloads and ratings, and every parent-lunchbox competitor is also at
zero, so the first twenty ratings decide who owns "school lunch planner". That is an ask to the
beta households, or a rating prompt in the app after the second week's plan (a product decision,
not a listing one).

Conversion (impressions → page views → downloads) is in App Store Connect → App Analytics →
Acquisition, free; the teaser's "about 20% open it, about 10% install" benchmarks are the
tool's, not Apple's, and unverified.

Popularity scores for the shortlist above come free from an Apple Search Ads account (keyword
recommendations show Search Popularity without spending), which is what the paid tools resell.

## Method

Apple lookup and search API (itunes.apple.com/lookup, /search, entity=software, country=us,
limit=200), 40 terms, 3 s apart; subtitles read from each app's apps.apple.com page; Apple's
search guidance at developer.apple.com/app-store/search/. Apple's autocomplete endpoint
(MZSearchHints) now returns empty and was not used.
