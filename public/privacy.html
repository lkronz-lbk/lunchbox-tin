Privacy

Short version: signed out, the planner keeps everything on your device. Signed in, your household's lunches are also stored on our servers so a second phone can share them, and the only things we hold are your email address and that household. Like every website, the company that hosts it keeps ordinary server logs (the address your connection comes from, your browser type) for a short time.

The planner

Everything you enter — lunchbox names, food lists, school rules, plans, shopping ticks — is stored in your own browser's local storage on the device you used. Until you sign in, it is never sent anywhere. Clearing your browser data or deleting the installed app deletes it too. The planner's Setup screen has a copy-out button so you can keep your own backup or move to another phone.

Signing in

You can sign in with an email address so the same household works on more than one phone. There is no password: we email you a link that works once. Signed in, a copy of your household — the same data described above — is stored on our servers (hosted by Netlify, database by Neon, both in the United States) and shared only with the people you invite into it. We keep your email address, the household, and the sign-in sessions for your phones; nothing else. To limit sign-in abuse we keep a hashed form of the connection address a request came from, for one day. Signing out leaves the copy on your phone in place. Leaving a household leaves the lunches with it. Delete my account, in Setup, removes the household from our servers and from that phone and cannot be undone; if someone else you invited was in it, their sign-in ends too, and whatever their phone held stays on their phone. A helper you invite sees only the plan and the pack list, never the school rules, allergens or anyone's email address.

The email form

If you submit the form on the home page, your email address is stored by Netlify, which hosts this site, so I can email you when the features mentioned there are ready. It is not sold, shared, or used for anything else, and every email includes a way to unsubscribe. Ask at the address below and I'll delete it.

Analytics and tracking

There are no analytics scripts, no advertising pixels, and no third-party cookies on this site. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means Google receives the request for the font files; nothing else about you is sent with it. If you open the planner inside the Claude app rather than in a browser, a “Save a backup file” button appears in Setup; it hands your lunches to that app’s own save dialog, and nowhere else.

Children's information

The planner is set up and run by an adult. The one part a child touches is the “kid’s pick” screen, which shows two pictures at a time and never asks them for anything. A child's name and any allergens you enter are personal information about a minor: they stay on your device unless you sign in, in which case they are stored with the rest of the household as described above, never shared beyond the people in it, and never used for anything else. You are welcome to use initials or a nickname instead — the app never needs a real name to work.
