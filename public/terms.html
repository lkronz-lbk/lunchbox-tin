Paying

The Household plan is sold yearly or monthly, and is bought one of two ways. A plan bought at a founding price renews at that price for as long as it stays subscribed, bought either way; it ends if the plan is canceled and runs out.

On this website. Prices are shown before you pay, in US dollars, plus any sales tax or VAT that applies where you live. Payment is taken by Stripe on Stripe’s own page; we never see or store your card. A yearly plan renews automatically at the end of each year, and a monthly plan each month, at the price then shown unless it was bought at a founding price; we email you before a yearly plan renews. Cancel any time from Manage billing under Account → Subscription.

In the iPhone app. The plan is an in-app purchase through the App Store, charged to your Apple Account at the App Store’s price, and Apple takes the payment under its own terms. A yearly or monthly plan bought there renews automatically until you cancel it, in Manage in the App Store under Account → Subscription, or in your iPhone’s Settings.

Either way a canceled plan runs to the end of the period you paid for and does not renew, and it is changed where it was bought. If a renewal payment fails it is tried again for a while and the app tells you; if it keeps failing the plan ends and the household drops back to the free plan with everything still there.

Bought in the iPhone app: Apple took the payment, so only Apple can refund it; ask at reportaproblem.apple.com. A refund Apple gives ends the plan it paid for.

Your household

Whoever creates a household owns it and can invite, remove, and delete. Anyone you invite as a parent can change the lunches and buy the Household plan; the owner and whoever paid can manage or cancel a plan bought on this website, and a plan bought in the iPhone app is managed by the Apple Account that paid; a caretaker can only check off the pack list. Deleting the account removes the household from our servers and cannot be undone, so export a copy first if you want one. Deleting also ends a plan bought on this website at once. It cannot cancel a plan bought in the iPhone app, because Apple holds that subscription: cancel it in your iPhone’s Settings first, or Apple goes on charging for it. The lunches, names and rules you enter are yours; we store them to run the service and for nothing else, as the privacy policy describes.

You remain responsible for what goes in the box. For a child with a medical diet or a serious allergy, treat the plan as a starting point and check every item yourself.
