Questions, answered

Short answers to what parents ask. Anything else: hello@lunchsorted.app.

How does it pick the week?

From the foods on your list, matched so each box goes together: something crunchy against a soft main, protein when the main is light, something tangy to cut the salt. Each compartment shows the words the pairing used. It is not a random draw, and it never rewrites a day that has already gone.

How do I change one thing?

On Week, tap the compartment (the small swap arrows). Pick another food, or shuffle just that one. Tap a compartment and choose "Don’t change this one" to keep it through the next shuffle. "Shuffle" on a day redraws the day; "Shuffle all" redraws the week.

Why did a food stop showing up?

A school rule or an allergen you set under the gear beside the lunchbox name is keeping it out. The Foods tab marks each food it skips and says which rule. Change the rule, or remove the food.

Where does the shopping list come from?

Every planned box, as what to buy: a turkey and cheese pinwheel goes on the list as deli turkey, cheese slices and tortillas, grouped by aisle, with a count when two dishes share a thing. Tick what the pantry already has. Copy puts it on the clipboard; on a phone, the share button sends it to Notes, Reminders or a text.

How does packing work?

Pack shows the next school day’s box with a flag when it needs an ice pack, a sealed container, or is missing protein. One tick marks the box packed. The morning after, it asks what came home, and the next week leans toward what actually gets eaten.

What is kid’s pick?

Under the gear, switch on "They pick their box each day". The night before, hand them the phone: part by part, two pictures at a time, or the whole box against the next one. Either way the choice comes from the week you already shopped for, so nothing new to buy. Add a photo of the real thing beside a food for a child who cannot read yet. The iPhone app can remind you at the time you choose.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything stays on your phone until you sign in. Sign in with your email, no password, to share the week with the other parent, give a helper the pack list, or keep a backup. Delete the account any time from the Account tab.

What is free, and what does the plan cost?

Planning the week, the shopping list and the pack list are free for good, for one lunchbox. Kid’s pick, the morning review, a pantry that remembers, more lunchboxes and sharing are the Household plan. Every new household gets the whole plan for three weeks, no card. Prices are on the front page; refunds within 14 days, no questions.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once opened, the planner works without a network. Changes sync when you are back online if you are signed in.

Something is wrong. Where do I say so?

Tap the ? at the top of the app and "Ask a question", which opens an email with the details we need already filled in, or write to hello@lunchsorted.app.
