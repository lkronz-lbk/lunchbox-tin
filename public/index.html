What's in it

Four jobs.

Main

It knows what goes together

Not a random draw. Crunch against a soft main, protein when the main is light, something tangy to cut the salt — and it tells you why each box was built that way. Tell it what came home uneaten and next week’s draw leans toward what actually gets eaten.

Side

Your school's rules, respected

Nut-free, seed-free, dairy-free, cold-only, no ice pack, a short eating window, no chocolate — plus anything else you list. Excluded foods are flagged in your list, never silently dropped, so you always know why something stopped appearing. Add a snack or drink compartment if your school day needs one.

Fruit

The shopping list writes itself

Every planned box rolls into one list grouped by aisle, with quantities. Tick off what the pantry already has and shop the rest. More than one kid? One list covers them all.

Sweet

Mornings become a checklist — and kids get a say

The next school day as a checklist, with a flag when the box needs an ice pack, a sealed container, or is missing any protein. The night before, hand them the phone: a couple of pictures per compartment, all inside your rules, and they tap what they want. A lunch they chose tends to come home emptier.

On your phone

Built for standing at the kitchen counter.

Designed at phone size first, because that's where lunch actually gets planned.

Install it

It can live on your home screen.

There is no app to download. Add it to your home screen and it opens full-screen with its own icon, works with no signal, and keeps everything on the device.

  1. iPhone: open the planner in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  2. Android: open it in Chrome, tap the menu, then Install app.
  3. Open it from the icon after that — installed, it holds onto your plans far more reliably than a browser tab.
